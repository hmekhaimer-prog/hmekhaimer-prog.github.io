About

The observer behind the observations.

I write because I notice things. Not extraordinary things. The kind of things everyone sees but few pause long enough to name: how a city speaks through its walls, how power disguises itself as convenience, how identity shifts quietly beneath the surface of routine.

I am not a philosopher. I am not a journalist. I am someone who travels, observes, and refuses to let experience pass through him without leaving a mark. Writing is how I process. Photography is how I remember. Together, they form a practice of paying attention in a world that rewards distraction.

What This Space Is

This is a space for slow reflection in a fast world. Each piece begins with a place, a moment, or an image, and follows it wherever it leads. Sometimes that means gentrification. Sometimes it means identity. Sometimes it means the politics of visibility. I do not plan the destination before I begin walking.

I write long form because certain ideas cannot survive compression. If a thought can be captured in a headline, it was probably never a thought. The pieces here require time. They reward patience. They are not for everyone, and that is by design.

I do not write to inform. I write to make the familiar feel strange again, so that something new becomes possible in the gap between what you assumed and what you now see.

What I Believe

Observation Over Opinion

The world does not need more opinions. It needs more people willing to look carefully before speaking.

Depth Over Frequency

One piece that changes how you see is worth more than fifty that confirm what you already believe.

Authenticity Over Performance

I share what I genuinely think, not what algorithms reward. The audience I want is the one that stays for substance.

Controlled Visibility

Not everything needs to be revealed. Powerful writing gives enough to create recognition and leaves enough unspoken to sustain curiosity.

The Person

I am based in Cairo, Egypt. I work in operations by profession. I write and photograph by compulsion. The tension between structured work and creative chaos is not accidental. It is the same tension I write about. Systems, efficiency, control on one side. Art, rebellion, spontaneity on the other. I live in the space between.

Every city I enter becomes a mirror. I travel not to escape, but to see myself in unfamiliar light. Berlin was the first city I wrote about. It will not be the last.
